# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sexual
conduct without consent, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
contact method listed in the repository security policy. Do not include
sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when behavior violates this policy.
