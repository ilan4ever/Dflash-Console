# DFlash Console trademark policy

This policy explains the branding rights that are separate from the
copyright license for DFlash Console.

## Protected marks

“DFlash”, “DFlash Console”, the DFlash Console logo, and related names,
logos, and visual identifiers are project branding owned or controlled by
ILAN AVIV where applicable. The GNU Affero General Public License does not
grant permission to use these marks.

## Allowed references

You may make truthful, descriptive references such as:

- “Based on DFlash Console”
- “Compatible with DFlash”
- “A fork of DFlash Console”

Keep the reference clear and do not suggest that your project is an official
release, endorsed by, or maintained by the DFlash Console maintainers.

## Forks and modified distributions

Modified distributions should use a distinct product name, application
identity, icons, splash screens, update channels, and domain names. Do not
remove or alter the DFlash copyright and license notices. Do not use the
DFlash marks as the primary brand of a modified product without written
permission.

An unmodified distribution may retain its original DFlash branding when the
AGPL and other applicable terms are followed.

## Third-party marks

Names such as Hugging Face, LM Studio, Electron, DFlash, llama.cpp,
whisper.cpp, Piper, CUDA, and model or runtime names belong to their
respective owners. DFlash Console is independent unless an explicit
partnership is stated.

For permission to use DFlash branding beyond these guidelines, contact the
maintainer before publishing the distribution.
